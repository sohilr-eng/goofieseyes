Privacy

goofieseyes is a photography and film studio. This page says what we collect when you use goofieseyes.live, buy a print, or talk to our Facebook, Instagram and Threads accounts, what we do with it, and how to have it deleted. Questions go to goofiesfotos@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

When you send an enquiry

The contact form sends us your name, email address, the kind of work you're asking about and your message. It arrives as an email (delivered by Resend) so we can reply. Nothing else is stored on the site.

When you buy a print or a digital file

Checkout is run by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach us. Stripe passes us your name, email address, shipping address (for physical prints) and what you bought, so we can make, ship or send your order and answer questions about it.

When you browse the site

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits: which pages are viewed, the referring site, browser and device type, and country. It sets no cookies and doesn't follow you to other sites.

Meta Pixel and Conversions API

We are adding Meta's advertising tools to the site so we can tell whether our Facebook and Instagram ads lead to sales, and show ads to people likely to be interested. Once they are on:

  • The Meta Pixel uses cookies to record visits and actions such as viewing a print, starting checkout and completing a purchase, along with your browser and device details.
  • When you buy something, our server may also send Meta the order value and currency, plus your email address and shipping country in hashed (one-way scrambled, SHA-256) form. That's the Conversions API, which lets Meta match the purchase to a Meta account without us sending readable contact details.
  • Meta uses this under its own data policy, including to measure and improve ads.
  • Where the law requires your consent, the Pixel won't load until you've given it. You can also block it with your browser's cookie settings, and control how Meta uses off-site activity in your Meta ad preferences.

Our social accounts and the goofieseyes studio app

We run our own Meta app, goofieseyes studio, to manage our own Facebook Page, Instagram and Threads accounts. It publishes our posts, reads statistics about them (reach, likes, follower counts) and lets us read and reply to comments and messages people send those accounts. Through it we see what any account owner sees: your public name, username, profile picture, and what you wrote to us. We use that only to reply to you and to understand which of our posts work. The app doesn't access anyone's account but ours, and we never sell or share what it shows us.

Posts we've scheduled, and the photos and videos in them, are stored with our hosting providers until they're published. Uploaded media is deleted from our storage about two days after it goes live.

Who it's shared with

Only the services that run the site for us, for those purposes: Vercel (hosting, analytics, media storage), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Upstash (scheduling and storage for the studio app) and Meta (as described above). We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiry emails: as long as the conversation or job needs, then deleted on request.
  • Order records: as long as tax and accounting rules require, usually seven years.
  • Analytics: aggregate counts only.
  • Studio uploads: about two days after publishing.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your data

Email goofiesfotos@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" (or "Copy of my data") and the email address or social account it concerns. We'll confirm within 30 days. If you've messaged one of our social accounts, deleting it on our side doesn't remove it from Meta's systems. You can delete your own comments and messages there, and Meta's tools cover the rest.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the UK or the EU), you may have further rights, such as objecting to how we use your data or complaining to a regulator. The same email address is the way to use them.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
